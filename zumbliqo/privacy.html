ZUMBLIQOPrivacy Policy & Data Deletion

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

ZUMBLIQO is a digital media channel producing short-form international visual news videos (Visual News Pro). This page explains what data our internal publishing tool Zumbliqo Publisher processes across Meta platforms (Facebook and Instagram) as well as YouTube, Bluesky, and Discord, and how to request data deletion.

1. Controller and Contact

Responsible project: ZUMBLIQO.
Contact email for privacy inquiries and data deletion requests: zumbliqo@gmail.com.

2. Scope of Zumbliqo Publisher (Internal Tool Only)

Zumbliqo Publisher is a private, internal automation tool operated exclusively by the ZUMBLIQO owner to publish ZUMBLIQO's own videos to ZUMBLIQO's own official accounts. It is not offered to third-party users, does not provide public user login, and does not access any third-party Facebook, Instagram, or YouTube account.

3. Meta Graph API (Facebook Reels & Instagram Reels)

Using the official Meta Graph API with permissions explicitly granted by the ZUMBLIQO Page and Instagram Business account administrator (pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_posts, instagram_basic, instagram_content_publish, business_management), Zumbliqo Publisher:

  • Uploads and publishes ZUMBLIQO's own short-form videos to the official Zumbliqo Facebook Page and the linked @zumbliqo Instagram Business account.
  • Reads basic own-account metadata (Page ID, Instagram Business Account ID, media count, and publishing quota limit) solely to verify connectivity and prevent duplicate uploads.

Zumbliqo Publisher does not read private messages, does not collect or store personal data about followers, viewers, or commenters, does not sell or transfer data to third parties, and does not use Meta data for advertising or profiling.

4. Other Connected Platforms

  • YouTube API Services: uploads ZUMBLIQO's own videos to the @ZUMBLIQO channel and reads own-channel upload metadata to prevent duplicates, subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
  • Bluesky & Discord: publishes ZUMBLIQO's own videos and links to ZUMBLIQO's official Bluesky account and Discord announcement channel.

5. Data Storage, Security, and Retention

  • Access tokens and account identifiers (Meta Page Access Token, YouTube OAuth refresh token, Bluesky app password, Discord webhook URL) are stored exclusively as encrypted secrets inside a private GitHub repository and used in memory during automated GitHub Actions runs. They are retained only until revoked or replaced.
  • Publication logs store only non-personal publication metadata (video title, SHA-256 hash, public post URL, public news sources, and media licenses) to enforce anti-duplicate checks and editorial traceability.
  • This static privacy page sets no cookies, uses no trackers, and collects no form data.

6. Revoking Access & Data Deletion Requests

Because Zumbliqo Publisher only accesses ZUMBLIQO's own accounts, no personal data of external users is collected or stored.

  • Revoke Meta access: Remove Zumbliqo Publisher at any time from your Facebook Business Integrations settings (facebook.com/settings?tab=business_tools).
  • Revoke Google/YouTube access: Remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Data deletion request: Send an email to zumbliqo@gmail.com with the subject Data Deletion Request. Any associated tokens or records will be permanently deleted within 48 hours and confirmed by email.

7. Updates

This policy may be updated if our publishing workflows change. The effective date is shown at the top of this page.
