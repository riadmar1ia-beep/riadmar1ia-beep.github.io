ZUMBLIQOPrivacy Policy & Data Deletion

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

ZUMBLIQO is a digital media channel producing short-form international visual news videos (Visual News Pro). This page explains what data our internal publishing tool ZUMBLIQO Publisher processes across TikTok, Meta (Facebook and Instagram), YouTube, Bluesky, and Discord, and how to revoke access or request data deletion.

1. Controller and Contact

Responsible project: ZUMBLIQO.
Contact email for privacy inquiries and data deletion requests: zumbliqo@gmail.com.

2. Scope of ZUMBLIQO Publisher (Internal Tool Only)

ZUMBLIQO Publisher is a private, internal automation tool operated exclusively by the ZUMBLIQO owner to upload ZUMBLIQO's own videos to ZUMBLIQO's own official accounts. It is not offered to third-party users and does not access any third-party TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, or YouTube account.

3. TikTok API (Content Posting API — Inbox Draft Upload)

Using the official TikTok Login Kit and Content Posting API with permissions explicitly granted by the @zumbliqo account owner (user.info.basic, video.upload), ZUMBLIQO Publisher:

  • Transfers ZUMBLIQO's own short-form MP4 videos to the @zumbliqo TikTok app Inbox as drafts (/v2/post/publish/inbox/video/init/) for manual review and final publishing inside the native TikTok application.
  • Maintains encrypted OAuth access and rotating refresh tokens solely to authenticate the @zumbliqo account.

ZUMBLIQO Publisher does not collect, store, or process data about TikTok followers, viewers, comments, or private messages, and does not sell or share TikTok data with any third party.

4. Meta Graph API (Facebook Reels & Instagram Reels)

Using the official Meta Graph API with permissions explicitly granted by the ZUMBLIQO Page and Instagram Business account administrator (pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_posts, instagram_basic, instagram_content_publish, business_management), ZUMBLIQO Publisher uploads and publishes ZUMBLIQO's own short-form videos to the official Zumbliqo Facebook Page and linked @zumbliqo Instagram Business account, and reads basic own-account metadata solely to verify connectivity and prevent duplicates.

5. Other Connected Platforms

  • YouTube API Services: uploads ZUMBLIQO's own videos to the @ZUMBLIQO channel, subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
  • Bluesky & Discord: publishes ZUMBLIQO's own videos and links to ZUMBLIQO's official Bluesky account and Discord announcement channel.

6. Data Storage, Security, and Retention

  • Access and refresh tokens are stored exclusively as encrypted secrets inside a private GitHub repository and used only in memory during automated GitHub Actions runs. Tokens are rotated securely and retained only until revoked or replaced.
  • Publication logs store only non-personal video metadata (title, SHA-256 hash, upload/post ID, public URL, public news sources, and media licenses) to enforce anti-duplicate checks and editorial traceability.
  • This static page sets no cookies, uses no trackers, and collects no form data.

7. Revoking Access & Data Deletion Requests

  • Revoke TikTok access: Open the TikTok app → Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions and remove ZUMBLIQO Publisher.
  • Revoke Meta access: Remove Zumbliqo Publisher at facebook.com/settings?tab=business_tools.
  • Revoke Google/YouTube access: Remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Data deletion request: Email zumbliqo@gmail.com with the subject Data Deletion Request. Any associated tokens or records will be permanently deleted within 48 hours.

8. Updates

This policy may be updated if our publishing workflows change. See also our Terms of Service.
